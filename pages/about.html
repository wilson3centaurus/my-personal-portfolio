Hi there! I'm Tafadzwa Wilson Sedze, a full-stack software engineer and First Class Computer Science graduate from Manicaland State University of Applied Sciences, based in Harare, Zimbabwe.

I build production software and the robots that run on it. I designed and maintain RoboCore, RoboKorda Africa's internal ERP, and build systems for schools such as an RFID staff attendance tracker. My core stack is Next.js, TypeScript and Supabase, with hands-on ESP32, Arduino and Raspberry Pi work.

As a Robotics and Coding Instructor, I've led student teams from national wins in Zimbabwe to international robotics competitions in Switzerland (2025), India (2025) and China (2026).
