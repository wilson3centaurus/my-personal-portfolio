About me

I'm Tafadzwa Wilson Sedze, a full-stack software engineer and First Class Computer Science graduate based in Harare, Zimbabwe.

I build production software and the robots that run on it. I designed and maintain RoboCore, RoboKorda Africa's internal ERP, and build systems for schools such as an RFID staff attendance tracker. My core stack is Next.js, TypeScript and Supabase, and I'm just as comfortable with an ESP32, an Arduino or a Raspberry Pi on the bench.

As a Robotics and Coding Instructor, I've taken student teams from national wins in Zimbabwe to international robotics competitions in Switzerland, India and China.
